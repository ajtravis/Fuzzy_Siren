The Studio

Why "Fuzzy Siren"?

A siren is a voice you can't ignore. A fuzzy one is a voice you can't ignore that is also, somehow, a cat. That tension between the mythic and the mundane, the serious and the silly, is where our games live.

Who we are

Fuzzy Siren LLC is an independent video game studio founded in 2026 and based in Central Ohio. We design, build and publish our own original titles for PC, with consoles to follow.

We're deliberately small. A lean studio can take creative risks a large one can't, keep its promises to players, and spend its budget on the game instead of the overhead around it.

What we believe

  • Atmosphere is a mechanic. Mood, sound and setting aren't dressing. They're part of how a game plays.
  • Scope is a feature. We'd rather finish something small and sharp than abandon something sprawling.
  • Respect the player. Fair prices, finished games, no manipulation.
  • Work in the open where it helps. We share progress when it's useful and keep quiet when it isn't.

Where we're headed

Our first title is in production. Beyond that: a studio that can sustain itself on a steady cadence of original games, each one better crafted than the last.
