Privacy policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Fuzzy Siren LLC ("Fuzzy Siren", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through fuzzysiren.com (the "Site"), how we use it, and the choices you have. It is written to be read, not skimmed past.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us. If you join our mailing list we collect your email address. If you use the contact form we collect your name, email address, and the contents of your message.
  • Technical information. Like most websites, our hosting provider may log standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, pages requested and timestamps for security and reliability purposes.

We do not use advertising trackers, and we do not sell personal information.

How we use it

  • To reply to your messages.
  • To send studio news to people who asked for it. Every email includes an unsubscribe link.
  • To keep the Site secure and working.

Cookies

The Site does not set tracking cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address as part of serving the font files; see Google's privacy policy.

Service providers

We rely on a small number of third parties to run the Site, such as our web host and a form/email delivery provider. They process data only on our behalf and only as needed to provide their service.

Retention

We keep contact messages for as long as needed to respond and maintain a record of the conversation. Mailing-list addresses are kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to remove them.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by emailing ajt@fuzzysiren.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA; we'll honor them.

Children

The Site is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be noted on the Site.
